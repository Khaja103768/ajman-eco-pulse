A citywide total can tell us how much waste is collected. But looking deeper reveals something else: communities can have distinctly different waste profiles.

Eco-Pulse turns those differences into something we can see, explore and compare — transforming open data into a clearer picture of Ajman's waste footprint.

Eco-Pulse was created exclusively from open datasets published through the Ajman Data platform.

District composition

03

Data validation

Original source files were preserved unchanged. Processed visualization files were generated separately after validation.

DISTRICT TOTALS 100%
04

Conflicting observations

Eight historical year-month combinations contained conflicting duplicate values. Three late-2019 pairs showed an exact 1,000× scaling discrepancy and were normalized consistently; the five unresolved months remain excluded and marked on the timeline.

ABOUT THE NUMBERS

Values are presented according to the published source data. The 2023 district composition should not be interpreted as current 2026 district conditions, and 2025 historical data represents available observations through September rather than a complete calendar year.
